Any 90s kid knows this game. Adventure Island was a staple of the era, and building a clone of it was a chance to go deep on something most developers skip — a game loop, collision detection, and sprite rendering, all without reaching for a ready-made engine.

This covers one chapter of the full game story. The character can move forward, backward, and jump; there's a specific set of rewards to collect and a bunch of enemies to avoid. All of it is built in **Java Swing**, which made the rendering and event-handling work more involved than a modern game framework would — and considerably more instructive.

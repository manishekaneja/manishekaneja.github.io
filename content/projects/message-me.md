The idea behind Message ME is simple: people share more honest thoughts when they don't have to attach their name to them. This app lets you collect feedback from your connections anonymously — share your link, and anyone who has something to say can say it without hesitation or fear of attribution.

The full stack is **Angular 9 with Angular Material** for the frontend, **Firebase** for the database and auth, and **Cloud Functions** for server-side logic. It was a natural fit for exploring a real-time, serverless architecture — and the anonymous feedback use case made it genuinely useful to build and test with real responses.
